# SEMS Pricing

Axon 1Pro Smart Estate Management System v2.0 (SEMS)

> Indicative prototype pricing only. Final pricing is subject to confirmed scope, integrations, infrastructure, resident/user volume and formal quotation.

## Standard Pricing

### Setup / Deployment
**SGD 4,000 one-time**

### Monthly Platform Subscription
- Up to 500 residents: **SGD 200 / month**
- 501–700 residents: **SGD 300 / month**
- 701–1,000 residents: **SGD 400 / month**
- Above 1,000 residents: **Custom Pricing**

### Annual Maintenance / Dedicated Infrastructure
**SGD 2,000 / year**

Baseline annual fee is intended to cover the agreed dedicated production environment/VPS allocation, maintenance and system updates for the client deployment.

Additional high-volume storage, custom hardware, third-party API usage, native app-store packaging, special infrastructure, major custom integrations and on-site services may be separately quoted.

Third-party API/provider subscriptions are excluded and are normally paid directly by the client unless otherwise agreed.

## Why Subscription-Based

SEMS is subscription-based because AI, security controls, integrations, browsers, APIs and estate-management technology evolve continuously.

The subscription model allows Axon to:
- maintain the application
- update integrations
- improve workflows
- apply security updates
- support new API/providers
- improve AI-assisted features
- avoid leaving the client with a custom system that becomes obsolete and expensive to maintain

## Existing Systems

SEMS is designed to integrate around existing systems.

Facility booking, gate/LPR, access control, CCTV, payment and other specialist tools do not need to be replaced unless there is a business reason.
