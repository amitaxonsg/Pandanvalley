Why Axon cannot responsibly issue a fixed quotation from the feature list alone

The requested functions describe what the system should achieve, but development cost is materially affected by how Pandan Valley currently operates, who uses each process, approval rules, exception handling, existing systems, security/privacy requirements, hardware already available, hardware that must be purchased, and third-party integrations.

AI-assisted development tools can reduce development time and coding cost, but they do not replace process discovery. Axon's objective is to introduce an application that is effective for Pandan Valley's actual operations rather than simply reproduce existing manual steps in digital form.
