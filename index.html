Axon Proposed Smart Estate Architecture

Resident Portal

Feedback, service requests, ticket tracking and contractor registration.

Vendor / Staff Mobile

Attendance, geofence, job reports, photos, patrol and NFC workflows.

Security Gate

QR validation, contractor entry, vehicle verification and exception alerts.

MA Dashboard

Live issues, vendor activity, SLA, demerits, reports and approvals.

AI Assist Layer

Classification, anomaly detection, summaries, image checks and management insights.

Audit & Reporting

Time-stamped history, evidence, exports, monthly reports and council review.

Recommended rollout

Phase 1 — Core OperationsVendor attendance, work reporting, resident tickets, admin dashboard.
Phase 2 — Access & EvidenceQR contractor passes, security gate workflow, photo verification, notifications.
Phase 3 — Physical AccountabilityNFC routes, geofence auto-check-out, stronger identity controls.
Phase 4 — IntelligenceSLA engine, AI insights, vendor scoring, analytics, exports and integrations.

Important MVP note

This is a concept demonstration using sample data. GPS tracking, facial recognition, NFC hardware, SMS/email delivery, secure QR validation, production AI, payment/SLA deductions and external-system integrations are simulated until the production backend and approved integrations are implemented.
