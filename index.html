BUSINESS VALUE

Today vs Proposed

Today

  1. Resident/council reports issue
  2. MA checks email / phone / Homeplus
  3. Contractor quotation through Tender Board
  4. Contractor activated manually
  5. Photos/messages scattered across channels
  6. Officer manually checks completion
  7. Reports compiled separately

Proposed with Axon

  1. Issue becomes structured ticket/work order
  2. Responsible vendor/team assigned
  3. Attendance and evidence linked to job
  4. Mandatory before/after + checklist
  5. MA officer sign-off captured
  6. SLA / demerit evidence created automatically
  7. Monthly management report generated from one data set
