AI usage in this module

Recommended policy: AI advises, summarises and flags. It should not autonomously approve work, deny gate access, apply contractual penalties, or send sensitive resident replies without an authorised staff review.

Hardware / site requirement

No dedicated AI hardware is assumed if SEA-LION is accessed through an approved cloud/API deployment. On-premise/private AI requirements would materially change infrastructure and pricing.

Commercial assumptions

AI development and recurring SEA-LION model/API usage should be priced separately. The current free trial API is for POC/development, limited to 10 requests per minute, and should not be treated as a production/commercial entitlement. Usage cost will depend on volume of images, messages and reports.
