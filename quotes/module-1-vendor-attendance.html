Vendor Attendance & Geo-Fenced Accountability

Individual worker attendance, GPS verification, identity checking, late-arrival alerts, re-entry tracking and month-end vendor attendance reporting.

INDIVIDUAL MODULE QUOTATION

Purpose

This module gives Pandan Valley a reliable attendance record for each vendor worker. Security currently records attendance; the proposed system digitises that process and adds location, time and exception controls.

Module Development PriceS$ __________To be completed after final scope confirmation

Scope of this module

Individual attendanceEach approved worker is recorded separately rather than only at vendor-company level.
GPS / geofenceCheck-in location is validated against the approved Pandan Valley boundary.
Identity verificationSupports the agreed identity method, including facial recognition if formally approved.
Late alerts30-minute grace period can be configured and exceptions routed to MA.
Exit / re-entryTracks temporary exit, re-entry and final checkout based on agreed rules.
Monthly reportingAttendance, late arrivals, exceptions and total on-site time by worker/vendor.

AI usage in this module

AI can flag repeated lateness, unusual attendance patterns, impossible location/time combinations and vendors with declining attendance compliance. AI findings remain advisory for MA review.
