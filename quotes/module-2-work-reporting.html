MCST 581 · Pandan Valley · Module Quote 02

Digital Work Orders & Before/After Evidence

MA-created work orders, mandatory before/after evidence, job-specific checklists, vendor submission and officer sign-off with a three-year evidence trail.

INDIVIDUAL MODULE QUOTATION

Purpose

This module addresses Pandan Valley's stated problem that contractors are not currently forced to submit consistent evidence, attendance, quantity/quality proof or officer sign-off.

Module Development PriceS$ __________To be completed after final scope confirmation

Scope of this module

Work order creationMA creates and assigns jobs to the appropriate vendor.
Checklist templatesDifferent job types can use different maintenance/inspection checklists.
Before evidenceMandatory in-app photo capture before work starts.
After evidenceMandatory photo and optional video after completion.
MA sign-offOfficer approves, rejects or requests rectification.
3-year evidenceApproved work evidence retained for the required period.

How the module works

MA creates jobScope, location, vendor, due date
→
Vendor receives jobMobile/PWA work list
→
Before photoTimestamp + job + location metadata
→
Work + checklistRequired steps completed
→
After photo/videoCompletion evidence submitted
→
MA sign-offApprove / reject / rectify / close
Process principle: Every completed job should end with evidence plus an accountable MA sign-off, creating a searchable project reference instead of scattered photos and messages.

Included deliverables

  • Work-order dashboard
  • Vendor mobile/PWA job view
  • Configurable checklist templates
  • Before/after photo workflow
  • Optional selected-job video workflow
  • MA inspection/sign-off
  • Rectification loop
  • Evidence history and reporting

Dependencies / confirmation required

  • Confirm categories and required checklist for each maintenance type
  • Confirm expected monthly job volume
  • Confirm likely video usage/size for 3-year storage calculation
  • Confirm who can edit after submission and audit-history requirements
  • Confirm whether tender-board references need to be linked

AI usage in this module

AI can compare before/after images, detect duplicate or reused evidence, summarise completed work, flag missing proof and build vendor performance insights. MA remains responsible for final quality approval.

Hardware / site requirement

Primarily uses vendor/staff smartphones with camera access plus MA office devices. No specialist hardware is assumed unless site conditions require it.

Commercial assumptions

Storage allowance and video retention will be priced after expected monthly media volume is confirmed. Specialist image-AI usage is quoted separately under the AI module.
