Purpose

This module converts measurable exceptions from the other modules into a transparent compliance record. It is designed to recommend contractual impact, not automatically take financial action.

AI can explain score changes, summarise vendor risk, identify trends and prepare council-ready narratives. The numerical contractual rules remain deterministic and auditable rather than being invented by AI.
